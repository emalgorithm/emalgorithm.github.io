Our paper "[OpenWhistle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34839)" has been accepted as a **Spotlight at NeurIPS 2026 (top 1.3% of submissions)**! 🐬 We introduce the largest open dataset of dolphin whistles: 180,000 whistles (114.3 hours) from five dolphins over five years, with detection and classification benchmarks to study dolphin communication. Explore the [project](https://sumbre-lab.github.io/openwhistle/) and our open [datasets and models](https://huggingface.co/collections/dolphinteam/neurips26-openwhistle).
