Our paper "[OpenWhistle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34839)" has been accepted as a **Spotlight at NeurIPS 2026 (top 1.3% of submissions)**! 🐬 We introduce the largest open dataset of dolphin whistles—a rich resource for studying dolphin communication. It contains 180,000 whistles (114.3 hours) recorded over five years from five dolphins, with benchmarks for whistle-type detection and classification. Explore the [project](https://sumbre-lab.github.io/openwhistle/) and our open [datasets and models](https://huggingface.co/collections/dolphinteam/neurips26-openwhistle).
